AI Product UX/UI Designer

AI MAXIMUS is looking for a UX/UI Designer to create clear, accessible experiences for AI-powered products. You will work with users, product managers, and developers to turn complex features into interfaces that are easy to understand and use.

Responsibilities:

  • Research user needs through interviews, usability sessions, and feedback analysis.
  • Map user journeys and create wireframes, interactive prototypes, and polished interface designs.
  • Design helpful loading, empty, error, and feedback states for AI-powered interactions.
  • Communicate uncertainty and give users clear ways to review, correct, or retry AI outputs.
  • Maintain reusable design components and collaborate with developers on implementation.
  • Test layouts across screen sizes and review accessibility, readability, and interaction details.

What we would like to see:

  • A portfolio showing your design process, decisions, and completed interface work.
  • Experience with Figma, prototyping, and reusable components.
  • Understanding of visual hierarchy, responsive layouts, and accessibility fundamentals.
  • Ability to plan simple usability tests and turn observations into design improvements.
  • Comfortable discussing design decisions with developers and product teammates.
  • Interest in AI products and awareness of how unpredictable outputs affect user experience.

Salary range and compensation:

Negotiable based on skills and experience.

Benefits:

  • Access to design and prototyping tools for project work.
  • Learning support for design courses, books, and workshops.
  • Regular design feedback and collaboration with engineering teams.
  • Flexible working arrangements agreed with the team.

Data Analyst

AI MAXIMUS is looking for a Data Analyst who can turn business questions into clear, useful insights. You will work with teams across the company to clean data, build reports and dashboards, and explain findings that support everyday decisions.

Responsibilities:

  • Gather and clean data from spreadsheets, databases, and internal business systems.
  • Write SQL queries to explore trends, compare performance, and answer business questions.
  • Build and maintain dashboards with clear metrics, filters, and definitions.
  • Check report accuracy and investigate inconsistent or missing data before sharing results.
  • Present findings with concise explanations, useful visualizations, and practical recommendations.
  • Document recurring analyses and automate reporting tasks where appropriate.

What we would like to see:

  • A degree in Statistics, Business Analytics, Computer Science, or equivalent practical experience.
  • Comfortable using SQL and spreadsheets to filter, join, summarize, and validate data.
  • Familiarity with a visualization tool such as Power BI, Tableau, or Looker Studio.
  • An understanding of descriptive statistics and the difference between correlation and causation.
  • Basic Python or pandas experience is a plus.
  • Clear communication skills and a portfolio of reports, dashboards, or analysis projects.

Salary range and compensation:

Negotiable based on skills and experience.

Benefits:

  • Learning support for analytics courses and professional workshops.
  • Access to reporting tools and structured feedback from experienced teammates.
  • Flexible working arrangements agreed with the team.
  • Opportunities to work with product, operations, and engineering teams.

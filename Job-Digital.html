Digital & AI Transformation Associate

Siam Smart Solution is looking to recruit a Digital & AI Transformation Associate to join our team in the heart of Bangkok (Sathorn). In this role, you will analyze existing workflows across various departments and identify opportunities to introduce No-Code/Low-Code AI automation tools, making business processes faster, highly accurate, and exponentially more efficient.

Responsibilities:

  • Audit traditional business workflows and design new, AI-driven processes that eliminate manual and redundant steps
  • Build and connect automated workflows using No-Code/Low-Code platforms (such as Zapier, Make.com, Microsoft Power Automate, etc.).
  • Gather, clean, and analyze data using AI models, creating intuitive dashboards (Power BI, Tableau, Google Sheets) for management decision-making.
  • Conduct workshops, training sessions, and produce internal documentation to foster an AI-ready culture and improve AI literacy.
  • Ensure all AI integrations protect user privacy and strictly comply with Thailand's PDPA and ethical AI frameworks.
  • Monitor implementation progress, calculate estimated ROI, and present transformation reports to executives.

What we would like to see:

  • Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, MIS, Data Science, Engineering, or related fields with a strong interest in digital technologies.
  • Solid understanding of applied AI, Generative AI tools, and AI literacy.
  • Proficiency in No-Code/Low-Code Automation Tools (Zapier, Make, Power Automate, etc.).
  • Competency in data analysis tools (Advanced Excel, Google Sheets, Power BI, or Tableau).
  • Growth mindset, adaptability, and change management skills.
  • Outstanding presentation and communication skills with the ability to explain complex tech concepts in simple terms.
  • Strong awareness of ethical AI practices and PDPA regulations.

Salary range and compensation:

30,000 -50,000 THB/monthly depending on skills and experience

Benefits:

  • High-performance workspace equipment (MacBook or equivalent high-spec laptop)
  • Unlimited learning budget on platforms like Coursera, Udemy, and top global institutions
  • Fully sponsored tickets to national tech, innovation, and AI summits
  • Comprehensive health insurance and annual check-ups
  • Travel and fuel allowance for corporate client visits
